04 · Projects

Selected projects

Adaptive Writing Assistive Sleeve for Real-Time Finger Force and Wrist Motion Support

A wearable assistive sleeve for real-time finger force monitoring and wrist motion support during handwriting. Integrates flex sensors, force sensitive resistors, an MPU6050 IMU, ESP32 and servo motors, with a Random Forest model for detecting handwriting imbalance.

ESP32 Flex Sensors FSR MPU6050 IMU Servo Motors Random Forest
Featured

MediScan AI – Machine Learning-Based Disease Prediction System

A web-based healthcare application built on a trained Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) model. Users select symptoms and receive predicted diseases along with relevant descriptions and precautions.

Python Machine Learning MLP Flask HTML CSS CSV Datasets

MediRoute India – AI-Powered Healthcare Navigator & Cost Estimator

An AI-powered decision support platform that converts patient queries into structured clinical pathways, hospital recommendations and cost estimates. Integrates Groq Llama 3.3 70B with ICD-10 and SNOMED-CT clinical mapping and healthcare coverage insights.

05 · Research & Publications

Published research

IEEE First Author

Non-Invasive Biosensor-Integrated Artificial Pancreas System for Smart Management of Diabetes Mellitus

Published in IEEE Xplore · 9th International Conference on Trends in Electronics and Informatics (ICOEI 2026) · IEEE Document Number: 11541757

This research explores a non-invasive approach to diabetes management that integrates biosensors with an artificial pancreas framework. The system focuses on continuous glucose monitoring and intelligent insulin management, aiming to make glycaemic control more responsive and less dependent on invasive sampling.
